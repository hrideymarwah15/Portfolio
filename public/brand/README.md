# Hridey Marwah monogram

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, with actual transparent backgrounds. The shipped assets are `hm-light.png` and `hm-dark.png`. The header, footer and favicon choose the corresponding file with the site's theme. The original generated files remain outside the project; the website references these local copies.

The mark is an original HM ligature. It uses overlapping arches and shared stems, with a satin ceramic treatment that belongs to the desk's material palette. The light mark is deep dusty lilac with a fine ivory edge. The dark mark uses porcelain ivory and a lilac central stem, with a graphite edge. These are separate rendered treatments of one identity, rather than a punctuation-based wordmark.

## Final prompt set

Base generation: “Create a single minimal personal monogram logo for Hridey Marwah, a developer and designer. A compact HM ligature constructed from two interlocking open architectural arches and three clean vertical stems, restrained Swiss typographic geometry softened with slightly rounded ends. One continuous balanced silhouette, generous negative spaces, legible at 28 pixels. Single solid charcoal ink, crisp vector-like edges. Transparent background; symbol only. No wordmark, full stop, brackets, flower, circle badge, gradient, shadow, 3D, mockup, extra text or variants. Original mark.”

Light treatment: “Edit this exact HM monogram for the LIGHT theme of a minimal aesthetic developer portfolio. Preserve the exact outline, overlapping arches, proportions and negative spaces. Refine the blunt terminals into a quieter softened geometric finish. Fill with restrained deep dusty lilac #695779 and a very thin warm ivory bevel along the top-left edges, suggesting satin ceramic. Clean, mature, simple, almost flat, legible at 32 pixels. No container, badge, wordmark, dot, extra objects or cast shadow. Transparent background; centered, occupying 88 percent of the square image.”

Dark treatment: “Edit this exact HM monogram for the DARK graphite theme. Preserve the exact outline, overlapping arches, proportions and negative spaces. Refine the terminals into a quieter softened finish. Use warm porcelain ivory #e8e3dc for the outer arches and muted lilac #b7a2ce for the central stem. A subtle satin graphite edge adds a tactile finish; keep the face almost flat, clean and mature, legible at 32 pixels. No chrome, glow, container, badge, wordmark, dot, extra objects or cast shadow. Transparent background; centered, occupying 88 percent of the square image.”

No third-party logo artwork was copied. The [Pentagram MIT Media Lab identity](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mit-media-lab) informed the principle of one coherent monogram system, not this mark's shape. [Vitsœ's design principles](https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/good-design) informed the restraint and functional material treatment.
